# PART 1: ENGLISH

## A. Grammar (Highest Priority)

**Parts of Speech**

Q1. In "She quickly finished her homework," which word is the adverb?
**A: "quickly"**

Q2. Identify the noun in: "Honesty is the best policy."
**A: "Honesty"**

Q3. In "He is a brilliant student," which word is the adjective?
**A: "brilliant"**

**Articles (a, an, the)**

Q4. Fill in the blank: "She is ___ honest woman." (a/an/the)
**A: an** (silent 'h', vowel sound)

Q5. Fill in the blank: "He is ___ European." (a/an/the)
**A: a** (starts with consonant sound /j/)

Q6. Fill in the blank: "___ Ganga is the longest river in India." (a/an/the)
**A: The** (used before names of rivers)

**Prepositions**

Q7. Fill in the blank: "She is good ___ mathematics."
**A: at**

Q8. Fill in the blank: "He is married ___ a teacher."
**A: to**

Q9. Fill in the blank: "The cat jumped ___ the table."
**A: onto / on** (context-dependent; "onto" for movement)

**Conjunctions**

Q10. Fill in the blank: "He was tired, ___ he kept working."
**A: but / yet**

Q11. Fill in the blank: "___ she was late, she still finished the exam."
**A: Although / Though**

**Subject–Verb Agreement**

Q12. "Each of the students ___ (has/have) submitted the assignment." Choose correct.
**A: has** ("Each" is singular)

Q13. "The committee ___ (is/are) divided in their opinion." Choose correct.
**A: are** (committee acting as individuals with differing views)

Q14. "Neither the teacher nor the students ___ (was/were) aware of the change." Choose correct.
**A: were** (verb agrees with the nearer subject, "students")

Q15. "One of the boys ___ (is/are) missing." Choose correct.
**A: is**

**Tenses**

Q16. Change to present perfect: "She finished her work."
**A: She has finished her work.**

Q17. Change to past continuous: "He ate dinner when I called."
**A: He was eating dinner when I called.**

Q18. Choose correct: "By next year, I ___ (will complete/will have completed) my course."
**A: will have completed** (future perfect)

Q19. Choose correct: "She ___ (is living/has been living) here since 2015."
**A: has been living**

**Active & Passive Voice**

Q20. Convert to passive: "The chef cooks the meal."
**A: The meal is cooked by the chef.**

Q21. Convert to passive: "They are building a bridge."
**A: A bridge is being built by them.**

Q22. Convert to active: "The letter was written by her."
**A: She wrote the letter.**

**Direct & Indirect Speech**

Q23. Convert to indirect speech: He said, "I am going to Delhi tomorrow."
**A: He said that he was going to Delhi the next day.**

Q24. Convert to indirect speech: She said, "I can swim."
**A: She said that she could swim.**

Q25. Convert to indirect speech: He asked, "Where do you live?"
**A: He asked where I/she/he lived.**

**Degrees of Comparison**

Q26. Convert to comparative: "This is a big house." → This house is ___ than that one.
**A: bigger**

Q27. Convert to superlative: "She is a good singer." → She is the ___ singer in the group.
**A: best**

Q28. Choose correct: "He is senior ___ me." (to/than)
**A: to** ("senior to," not "senior than")

**Modals**

Q29. Fill in the blank: "You ___ (must/can) submit the form by Friday — it's mandatory."
**A: must**

Q30. Fill in the blank: "___ (May/Must) I come in?" (polite request)
**A: May**

Q31. Fill in the blank: "If I were you, I ___ (would/will) accept the offer."
**A: would**

**Question Tags**

Q32. Add a question tag: "You are coming to the meeting, ___?"
**A: aren't you**

Q33. Add a question tag: "She doesn't like tea, ___?"
**A: does she**

**Singular/Plural**

Q34. Choose correct: "The news ___ (is/are) surprising."
**A: is** ("news" is always singular)

Q35. Choose correct: "Mathematics ___ (is/are) an interesting subject."
**A: is** (subject names ending in -s are usually singular)
