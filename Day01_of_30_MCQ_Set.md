## Section A: English — Tenses & Active/Passive Voice (Q1–25)

1. Change to present perfect: "He completes the report." → He ___ the report.
(A) completed (B) has completed (C) is completing (D) had completed
2. "By the time you arrive, I ___ (will finish/will have finished) cooking."
(A) will finish (B) will have finished (C) finish (D) am finishing
3. Choose correct: "She ___ (works/has been working) at this bank for ten years."
(A) works (B) has been working (C) is working (D) worked
4. Convert to past perfect: "He left before she arrived." → He ___ left before she arrived.
(A) has (B) had (C) was (D) is
5. Choose correct: "They ___ (play/were playing) chess when the power went out."
(A) play (B) were playing (C) had played (D) plays
6. Convert to passive: "The manager approved the proposal." 
(A) The proposal is approved by the manager (B) The proposal was approved by the manager (C) The proposal has approved the manager (D) The proposal approves the manager
7. Convert to passive: "They are painting the office."
(A) The office is painted by them (B) The office was being painted by them (C) The office is being painted by them (D) The office has been painted by them
8. Convert to active: "The cake was baked by my mother."
(A) My mother bakes the cake (B) My mother baked the cake (C) My mother is baking the cake (D) My mother had baked the cake
9. Choose correct: "The Constitution ___ (adopted/was adopted) in 1949."
(A) adopted (B) was adopted (C) adopts (D) is adopted
10. Choose correct future perfect continuous: "By next year, she ___ (will have been working) here for a decade."
(A) will work (B) will have worked (C) works (D) is working
11. Choose correct: "I ___ (have lived/lived) here since 2010."
(A) lived (B) have lived (C) live (D) am living
12. Choose correct: "When I reached the station, the train ___ (left/had left)."
(A) left (B) had left (C) has left (D) leaves
13. Convert to passive: "Someone stole my bicycle."
(A) My bicycle is stolen (B) My bicycle was stolen (C) My bicycle has stolen (D) My bicycle steals
14. Choose correct: "He ___ (is knowing/knows) the answer."
(A) is knowing (B) knows (C) know (D) has knowing
15. Choose correct: "Water ___ (boils/is boiling) at 100°C." (general fact)
(A) boils (B) is boiling (C) boiled (D) has boiled
16. Convert to passive: "They will complete the project by June."
(A) The project will complete by June (B) The project will be completed by June (C) The project is completed by June (D) The project has been completed by June
17. Choose correct: "She ___ (has gone/went) to the market; she'll be back soon."
(A) went (B) has gone (C) goes (D) was going
18. Choose correct: "The Earth ___ (revolves/is revolving) around the Sun."
(A) revolves (B) is revolving (C) revolved (D) has revolved
19. Choose correct: "He ___ (was writing/wrote) a letter when I entered."
(A) wrote (B) was writing (C) writes (D) has written
20. Convert to passive: "The teacher is explaining the lesson."
(A) The lesson is explained by the teacher (B) The lesson was explained by the teacher (C) The lesson is being explained by the teacher (D) The lesson has explained the teacher
21. Choose correct: "I ___ (will be traveling/travel) to Shillong next week."
(A) travel (B) will be traveling (C) traveled (D) have traveled
22. Choose correct: "She ___ (had already left/left already) when I called."
(A) had already left (B) left already (C) has already left (D) already leaves
23. Convert to passive: "Please close the door."
(A) The door is closed (B) Let the door be closed (C) The door was closed (D) Close the door is done
24. Choose correct: "By 2030, scientists ___ (will develop/will have developed) new vaccines."
(A) will develop (B) will have developed (C) develop (D) are developing
25. Choose correct: "He always ___ (arrives/is arriving) late." (habitual action)
(A) arrives (B) is arriving (C) arrived (D) has arrived
